# SCN-field-hedge-burrow: layout pack vs. setting cards

- **Architecture:**
  - Long meadow grass covers the top of the map.
  - A laid hawthorn hedge runs across the lower part, with the gate gap drawn as a thin top rail.
  - A hedgerow oak rises from the hedge line, and ploughed farmland is below.
  - There is a shade band on the field side of the hedge.
- **Deviations (DEF-p4-envconcept-603-a/h):**
  - **Major:** the burrow and its spoil fan are drawn about 3 m out in the field, not at the hedge foot.
  - The binders read like wire.
- **Lighting:** high sun with a narrow shade band along the hedge, as in LGT-field-hedge-burrow-01.
- **Style:** waking register with no ornament; the waking negatives addon was used. No people or animals.
- **Landmarks:** calibrated to the image, with the burrow at its drawn position.
