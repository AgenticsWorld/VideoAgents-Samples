  - The passage runs west: a raw earth stretch, then the brick-walled stretch with verdigris whiplash iron ribs, then limestone with a mosaic dado.

- **Style:** dream register, restrained. The ornament appears only in the built part of the passage, as the card specifies. No people or text.
- **Landmarks:** calibrated to the image.
