# SCN-little-door-passage: layout pack vs. setting cards

- **Architecture:** a true-scale plan. The hall floor strip and its mosaic border are at the right. A narrow straight stone passage runs west, with verdigris iron ribs and mosaic borders. Rose petals lie near the garden end. The garden arch at the left opens onto roses (upper left), lilies and sweet peas (lower left) and pale gravel in bright daylight. The ~3 m length matches the card.
- **Deviations (DEF-p4-envconcept-603-d):**
  - The neighbouring hall doors are drawn as oak slabs inside the masonry. Their landmarks follow the architecture instead and are not drawn at those positions.
  - The crimson curtain is drawn inside the passage.
- **Lighting:** bright daylight at the garden end and warm stone at the hall end, as in LGT-little-door-passage-01.
- **Style:** dream register (iron ribs, mosaic, July garden flowers). No people or text.
- **Scale:** the x4.5 build uses the same xy (see `orientation.scale_note`).
