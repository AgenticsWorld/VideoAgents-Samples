# SCN-pool-bank: layout pack vs. setting cards

- **Architecture:**
  - The still pool is at the left, with water-lilies.
  - Curving reed beds sit to the north and south, with trampled footprinted mud between them.
  - A fallen willow trunk with S-curved limbs lies on the shore.
  - A scuffed race circle marked with sticks is in the centre-right, and the wood edge is at the right.
  - There are no buildings or paths.
- **Environment:** purple loosestrife and cream meadowsweet appear, and there are no flag irises. This follows the environment card's seasonal guard; see DEF-p4-envconcept-602.
- **Lighting:** open afternoon light. In plan the shadows are kept minimal on purpose.
- **Style:** dream register carried only by flora and curves, with no built ornament, as CR-009 and CR-014 require. No people or animals.
- **Deviations:** DEF-p4-envconcept-603-g (the circle is about 8 m across; the trunk lies diagonally).
