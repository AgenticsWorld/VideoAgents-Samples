# SCN-pool-of-tears: layout pack vs. setting cards

- **Architecture:**
  - The long hall is drawn flooded, with clear aqua-green salt water over the flags and the mosaic border.
  - The glass table stands in the water, with the fan and gloves floating to its left.
  - Three broken shore steps with grass are at the south (right) end, with a reed clump before them.
  - There are dark tide-lines at the door bases and the entrance gap is in the east wall.
- **Flood extent:** water covers the whole floor up to the steps. This is an open conflict, DEF-p4-envconcept-601.
- **Lighting:** gold lamp reflections and ripple rings on the water, as in LGT-pool-of-tears-01.
- **Style:** dream register. No people or animals.
- **Deviations:** DEF-p4-envconcept-603-f. The curtain is not drawn, so it is not a landmark here. The image has its own frame, which is not the SCN-long-hall frame.
- **Scale:** the x12 tank build uses the same xy.
