Four-panel character reference sheet following the reference layout: three full-body views (front / side / back) left to right, and one large head-and-shoulders close-up filling the right column; the exact same character, outfit and proportions in every panel; plain background. The sheet is laid out like four photographs pasted side by side on one landscape board: three vertical gaps cut the image into four upright panels, each a separate photograph of the same single figure. In the three full-body panels the figure is drawn small enough to fit completely inside the panel, with clear space above the head and below the feet, a small patch of floor and a soft contact shadow under the feet; if any part of the figure leaves its panel the sheet is void.
