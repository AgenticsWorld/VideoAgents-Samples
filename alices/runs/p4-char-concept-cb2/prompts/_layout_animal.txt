Four-panel character reference sheet following the reference layout: three full-body views (front / side / back) left to right, and one large head-and-shoulders close-up filling the right column; the exact same character, outfit and proportions in every panel; plain background. The sheet is laid out like four photographs pasted side by side on one landscape board: three vertical gaps cut the whole image into four equal-width upright panels, and each panel is a separate small photograph of the same single animal. Panel 1: the whole animal seen from the front. Panel 2: the whole animal in exact side profile. Panel 3: the whole animal seen from behind. Panel 4, the right column: a large close-up of the animal's head and neck, facing the camera or turned slightly three-quarter. In each full-body panel the animal is scaled down by perspective so its entire body, feet and tail fit inside the upright panel with clear space around it, a small patch of plain floor and a soft real contact shadow beneath it. Never render the image as one single full-bleed picture.
