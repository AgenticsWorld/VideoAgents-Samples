Plan of the sunny far edge of a formal garden, a 26 by 15 metre area in an Art Nouveau dream after Alphonse Mucha, built as a real set. Near the top of the image, running the whole width, a tall clipped dark yew hedge seen from above as a thick flat-topped green band, with one arched gap cut through it about 70 percent from the left. Between the hedge and the ha-ha lies a strip of unmown long golden seeding grass with poppies, cornflowers and moon daisies, drifting in soft combed curves; in its centre a large oval hollow where the grass is pressed flat like a nest. About three quarters of the way down, a straight dry-stone ha-ha wall runs the whole width, seen only as its thin pale coping line with a narrow inlaid gold-and-pastel mosaic band; at its left end, on the wall line, a square limestone gate-pier seen from above as its square cap with a small weathered lichened stone griffin statue crouched on it inside a carved round halo disc. Below the ha-ha the ground is lower, rough pale grass that fades into bright hazy light toward the bottom edge. Warm low late-afternoon sun from the west-north-west (upper left), soft shadows toward the east.
