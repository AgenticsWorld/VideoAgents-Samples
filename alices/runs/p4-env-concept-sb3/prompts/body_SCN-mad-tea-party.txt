Plan of a 32 by 18 metre yard in front of a large farmhouse at the edge of an English wood, an Art Nouveau dream after Alphonse Mucha. Along the top of the image, across the middle half of the top edge, lies the roof of the long two-storey house: a very thick shaggy grey-brown long-straw thatch combed in flowing curves like fur, with two small round brick chimney tops close together on the centre of the ridge. The house's front wall line runs along the bottom edge of the thatch eave; the front of the house faces south toward the table. In front of the centre of the house front a stone doorstep, and just right of it a small square top of a tall-case clock standing on the grass. In the middle of the image, running left to right parallel to the house front, a very long mahogany dining table about 7 metres long, covered with a stained white damask cloth crowded along its whole length with Victorian teacups, saucers, teapots, milk-jugs and plates of bread-and-butter; mismatched chairs along both long sides, one big upholstered arm-chair at the left end, and three chairs pushed together at the right end. The trunk of a great horse-chestnut stands just above-left of the table's left end; its great curving boughs arch out over the table like open ribs with wide gaps of dappled light between them, so the whole table and chairs remain clearly visible from above. Grass worn to bare earth along the chair lines. Near the lower right a small round stone well-head in the grass. A worn footpath comes in from the lower left corner through dry sunlit summer grass toward the doorstep. The crowns of wood trees fill the left edge. Late-afternoon sun from the west (left), soft shadows toward the east.
