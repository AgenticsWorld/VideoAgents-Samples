Plan of a 40 by 22 metre entrance court of a formal walled garden, an Art Nouveau dream garden after Alphonse Mucha, built as a real set. The bottom (south) edge of the image is the high honey-coloured limestone garden wall, fully present along the whole bottom edge, seen only as its thick coped top edge; the garden ends at that wall line. About 30 percent from the left, a very small gap in the bottom wall line marks the tiny low door. A broad raked buff-gravel walk about 4 metres wide runs straight across the whole image from the left edge to the right edge at mid-height; at its right end a gilded wrought-iron arbour arch of whiplash scrolls spans the walk, seen from above as a thin curving gold frame, with clipped dark yews beyond it. Just above-right of the tiny door, below the broad walk, a round bed about 5 metres across holds a large old bush rose seen from above as a round crown covered in white roses, some blotched with wet red paint; its bed is edged with low green-and-gilt iron hoops; beside it two small open paint-pots and one knocked over, red paint splashed on the gravel. To the left of the rose bed a wooden wheelbarrow and spade on fresh-turned soil. To the right of the rose bed a huge empty round terracotta flower-pot, seen from above as a thick rim around a dark empty interior. Carpet-bedding beds edged with clipped box are cut in card-suit shapes: a heart-shaped bed at the upper left, a diamond-shaped bed at the upper middle, a club-shaped bed at the upper right, and a spade-shaped bed at the lower right below the walk, filled with solid blocks of red pelargonium, white alyssum and blue lobelia. Above the walk, about 60 percent across, a round Art Nouveau fountain with a lobed stone basin and a bronze lily-stem centre; a narrow gravel cross walk runs down from it to the broad walk, where a round gold-and-pastel mosaic roundel is set flush in the gravel at the crossing. Late-afternoon sun from the west (left), soft shadows toward the east.
