Photorealistic live-action fantasy film rooted in England in 1862, shot on 35mm film with anamorphic lenses, 16:9 widescreen. Real sets, real locations and real costumed actors in 1862 dress. Wonderland spaces are real built sets and set extensions in an Alphonse Mucha-inspired Art Nouveau design: flowing organic lines, floral and vine ornament, circular halo roundels, mosaic and stained glass, aged pastel-and-gold surfaces with patina and moss; the waking 1862 meadow stays plain period realism. Talking animals and fantastical creatures are photoreal CGI animals with naturalistic anatomy, real fur, feathers, skin and scales, lit by the same light and grounded with real contact shadows. Natural and practical light: sunlight, candlelight and oil lamps, soft haze with volumetric light shafts and soft colour through stained glass. Restrained palette of verdigris teal, slate grey, dusty rose, soft sage and lilac pastels and tarnished-gold accents, warmed by amber sun and candle glow. Fine film grain, gentle halation, soft depth of field, grounded and quietly melancholy.
